# Guides to the Lean proof of the Riemann hypothesis

*As of 26 Nov 2026. The original guide entries record the versions read.*

## Sernau readable {#sernau-readable}

The upstream guide gives a reading order for `grh/` at `72fead10`. It follows the argument through `New/` and points to background results in `Lib/`. The release credits Claude for the Lean text and documentation. Andrew Sutherland’s shorter-edition verification is a separate record for both `grh/` and `grh-compact/`. [Pinned upstream guide](https://github.com/lsernau/grh-lean/blob/72fead102f663d5c7a407964a13dc0cf9d977207/grh/GUIDE.md) · [Readable source route](/sernau/route/) · [MIT shorter-edition checks](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/shortgrh/)
